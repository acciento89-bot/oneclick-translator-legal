Support / Spielhilfe

Wähle im Team ein Monster und tippe auf ein unteres Arenafeld. Drei identische Monster derselben Sternstufe lassen sich zu einem stärkeren Monster verbinden. Sechs Monster können gleichzeitig kämpfen. Jede Synergie verstärkt die Gruppe ab drei passenden Monstern.

Deploy a squad on the lower arena tiles, merge three matching species and stars, and conquer 60 campaign stages. Earn tickets from first clears and buy exact monsters with battle gold. Energy recovers every 10 minutes, up to 10.

Interrupted battles and summon reveals can be resumed. Purchases and ads need a connection and store availability. Use Settings to mute music, change haptics, reduce motion or revisit ad privacy. Restore Purchases applies to permanent entitlements; currency is local.

Base summon odds: Common 65%, Rare 25%, Epic 8%, Legendary 2%. Each rarity contains five equally likely species. At 20 summons without Epic-or-better: Epic 98%, Legendary 2%. At 80 without Legendary: Legendary 100%. Gold recruitment grants the exact displayed monster and does not change pity counters.

Premium Conquest Pass is a permanent 30-tier track without subscription or seasonal reset. No real-money prizes, cash withdrawals or multiplayer leaderboard are offered.
