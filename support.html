Before contacting support

  • Use /language to choose a target language.
  • Ask an administrator whether the channel was enabled with /setup add.
  • Check whether the bot is online and can view and send messages.
  • Never send passwords, bot tokens, API keys, or payment credentials.

Data access or deletion

Email acciento89@gmail.com with the subject OneClick Translator Data Request. Include your Discord user ID, the relevant server ID, and whether you request access, correction, or deletion.

Response time

Support requests are handled as soon as reasonably possible. Privacy requests are handled within applicable legal time limits.
